The film looks in this folder are 3D LUTs made by scripts/generate-film-looks.py with
Spectral Film LUT by Jan Lohse, which simulates each film stock from its digitized datasheet:
https://github.com/JanLohse/spectral_film_lut (commit a15043f6fe2d365d0c359d22e0868076800916d9)

Kodak, Fujifilm, Agfa and the stock names are trademarks of their owners. OpenPixels is not
affiliated with or endorsed by them; the names only say which stock a look simulates.
